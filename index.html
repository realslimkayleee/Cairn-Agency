The Cairn System

Not a chatbot. The system that runs your front office.

Five capabilities built as one connected system. Each hands off to the next — capture to quote, recover to book, book to follow-up — so no inquiry falls through the gaps.

01

Capture & sort every inquiry

Every call, form, and message answered in seconds and sorted: residential, commercial, or industrial; one-time or recurring; by size and frequency — so your best accounts reach you first.

02

Missed-call text-back

An unanswered call instantly fires a text, so the job you paid to generate doesn't go to the company down the road.

03

English + Spanish voice agents

Natural phone agents that answer questions, give ballpark pricing, qualify, and book — on brand, around the clock.

04

Instant quote & booking

Collects job details conversationally, gives a price range inside your rules, books the walkthrough or clean, and writes it to your calendar and CRM. No double entry.

05

Follow-up that turns one-offs into recurring

Polite, persistent sequences that turn a one-time clean into a standing account, rebook lapsed customers, and ask for the review at the right moment.
